## About Me

_I'm a Product Designer and Design Engineer who takes products from the first idea all the way to production. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Industrial Product Design from the University of Bologna and a Master's degree in Design and Innovation from the Technical University of Denmark (DTU). Since 2018 I have worked hands-on across very different industries: outdoor lighting and urban furniture, compact living spaces on wheels, luxury yacht interiors, outdoor equipment, and industrial, pharmaceutical and medtech products._

_Today I work as a Robust Design Engineer at RD8, a Copenhagen engineering consultancy. There I help international companies make their products more reliable, easier to manufacture and more cost-efficient. In parallel, I co-founded NeoCamp, where I designed, built and field-tested our own camping product._

_What ties my work together is a balance between creativity and engineering precision. I sketch, model and prototype to explore ideas quickly, and I use CAD, simulation and structured risk analysis to make sure those ideas hold up in real production and real use. I do my best work in cross-functional teams where design, engineering, production and business meet, and in environments that call for creativity, flexibility and close attention to detail._

## Work Experience

**Robust Design Engineer and Engagement Consultant @ [RD8](https://rd8.tech/about) (_Oct 2023 – now_)**

RD8 is an engineering consultancy specialised in robust design: making products perform reliably despite natural variation in parts, materials, assembly and use. I support international clients in several industries, including pharmaceutical and medical device companies, in developing and improving mechanical products.

My engineering work includes:
- Reviewing product and component designs to improve quality, reliability and manufacturability, applying robust design and Six Sigma principles
- 3D CAD modelling and design improvement of components and assemblies
- FEM simulation to validate features and design choices before they reach production
- Tolerance stack-up analysis and kinematic analysis to understand how variation affects product performance
- DFMEA risk analysis to identify and address failure modes early in development
- Technical documentation and industrialisation support for high-volume manufacturing, in close collaboration with clients' production and supply chain teams

Alongside the engineering work, I am responsible for client engagement activities:
- Customer management and day-to-day contact with client teams
- Designing the customer experience (CX) of how RD8 works with its clients
- Running training sessions and workshops for client engineers on robust design, kinematic analysis and tolerance stack-up analysis

**Co-founder and Head of Development @ [NeoCamp](https://www.neocamps.com) (_Apr 2023 – Aug2025)**

NeoCamp is a startup I co-founded that offers a B2B rental service for camping equipment. Besides building the company and shaping its business model and offer, I led the development of our own product, Rami, from concept to working product.

I designed and built Rami and tested it for the first time at Roskilde Festival 2023. From there, I refined it through three iterative development cycles between 2023 and 2025. Each cycle combined 3D modelling, functional prototyping and field validation, and used data and feedback from real users to improve performance, usability and manufacturability. The project gave me end-to-end experience of developing a physical product with limited resources, where every design decision also has to make business sense.

**Future Experience Designer @ [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/articles/2022/business-transformation-guide.html) (_Sep 2022 – Feb 2023_)**

I was part of the Future Experiences team within KPMG's Business Transformation service line. We worked with medium and large Danish companies on the holistic analysis of their services and organisational systems, with the goal of creating, developing and implementing new products, projects and programmes.

My work covered both research and design:
- User research and analysis to understand customer and employee needs
- UX mapping of customer journeys and service flows
- Future thinking to explore emerging technologies and market trends and their impact on clients' businesses
- Co-creation workshops with client stakeholders from different departments
- Prototyping and UX/UI design of digital products in Figma and Adobe XD, testing designs with end users and iterating based on their feedback

A central part of the role was turning research insights into concrete requirements and recommendations that client teams could act on.

**Executive Interior Designer and Engineer @ [Pad Project](https://www.linkedin.com/company/pad-project/about/) (_May 2019 – Aug 2020_)**

Pad Project is a design studio in Fano, Italy, specialised in luxury yacht interiors. I designed and engineered complex custom furniture systems for yachts of around 40 metres. On one project, I designed the owner's areas on the main deck and engineered the crew areas on the lower deck, where space, function and regulations all had to be balanced.

My responsibilities included:

- Guiding and managing carpenters, constructors and craftsmen through the production process

The role taught me to work with very high attention to detail, under tight deadlines, and with many stakeholders at the same time.

**Junior Product Designer @ [Neri](https://www.neri.biz/it/) (_Apr 2018 – Jul 2018_)**

Neri is an Italian company producing outdoor lighting and urban furniture, with in-house production. As part of the R&D team, I developed concepts for new lighting products and urban furniture, from research and sketches to 3D CAD models and renders, and supported the engineering of the products.

My work included:

This experience gave me a solid understanding of lighting products, both the quality of light and the technical components behind it, and of how design choices connect to production.

## Education

**M.Sc. Design and Innovation @ Technical University of Denmark (DTU), Copenhagen (_Sep 2020 – Mar 2023_)**

An engineering master's degree combining technical product development with innovation, management and business. Relevant courses included Design for Robustness, Design of Plastic Products, materials and production processes, product and project management, innovation management, behavioural design, business model design and sustainable product development. Many courses were based on real projects developed together with Danish companies.

In my thesis, _"Sustainability by Design: Advancing Innovation Practices at DTU Biosustain"_, I worked as a consultant for the Sustainable Innovation Office of the Novo Nordisk Foundation Center for Biosustainability. I analysed how researchers develop new biotechnologies, especially on the path from PhD research to startup, and identified methods and tools that could make their innovation more sustainable. I delivered the results as a report and summary booklet and presented them to the institution's Executive Management Team.

**B.Sc. Industrial Product Design @ University of Bologna (UNIBO), Italy (_Sep 2015 – Jul 2018_)**

A degree in product design covering creative and technical product development, production processes and technologies, communication and business creation. In my thesis, I designed and prototyped a street-light module for Neri S.p.A., which gave me my first real manufacturing experience and a lasting passion for lighting products.
